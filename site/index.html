Mission

Make AI-assisted work verifiable.

AI can now write good code, fast. What it cannot do is vouch for its own work, or tell you what you did not know to ask for. "All tests pass", from the agent that wrote the code, is a claim, not evidence.

So you keep your own AI builder and add two checks from outside it. AsBuild checks each change against tests you approved, before you merge. AsChecked reads the whole project and lists what a professional would fix first. The rules we build by are published as the Hevelius playbook.

How it fits

Two checks, at two moments.

Product

AsBuild

Free and open source · MIT licence

Your AI writes the code. AsBuild checks it against the tests you approved, before you merge.

Every requirement you agree gets a test that fails before the change and passes after it. A program on GitHub runs those tests and puts a plain receipt on the pull request: PROVEN, NOT PROVEN, WAITING FOR YOU or UNRESOLVED. It is maker-checker, the control banks use, applied to AI: your agent builds, a program checks, and you approve.

What you need

GitHub, Claude Code, and a Python project tested with pytest or a React app made with Vite.

For people who merge code written by an AI agent and answer for it.

Product

AsChecked

Free and open · CC BY 4.0

You built something with AI. AsChecked reads it and tells you, in plain words, what a professional would fix first.

You run it in the AI tool you already use. It is written only to read your project. You get at most five findings, most harmful first, each with its evidence, a fix and a prompt you can paste to your AI builder.

What you need

The AI tool you already use. Tested in Claude Code. It also runs in a claude.ai chat, not yet proven to give the same results.

For people who built something with AI and want to know what to fix first.

Advisory

AI adoption, from first assessment to working practice.

For organizations that want AI to deliver, and need to know where to start, what will change, and how to govern it.

  • AI readiness assessment

    A clear view of where you stand: use cases, data, skills and controls. You receive a short written assessment with priorities, and a direct answer on what to do first.

  • Transformation

    We turn a chosen use case into a working process: the operating model, the roles, and the path from a small pilot to wider use.

  • Strategy and governance

    AI strategy, the business case, and governance mapped to published frameworks such as the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. We provide evidence and advice, not certification.

  • AsChecked hand review

    We run the AsChecked review on your project for you, and you get a short written report on what to fix first.
