Experience

The experience behind the company

  • Leadership. Led cross-functional teams of 5 to 10 people, directing designers, developers and analysts across multiple geographies, working directly with C-level executives.
  • HSBC Group. Created target operating models and IT implementation roadmaps for global business banking, and led early AI adoption across the bank: evaluating large language model (LLM) vendors, defining use cases and scaling adoption.
  • KPMG Advisory. Defined the operational processes, the customer outreach strategy and the design of a know-your-customer (KYC) platform for a global banking client, with a user base of more than 250,000 and 15,000 monthly active users.
  • Société Générale. Spearheaded the development and deployment of a vendor-based regulatory reporting platform for APAC and Japan, reducing the reporting cycle from days to hours.
  • Credit Suisse. Delivered a front-to-back transformation program focused on straight-through processing (STP) of listed and over-the-counter (OTC) equity derivatives.
  • Barclays. Designed the controls and operational processes for reconciling trading books under the Unauthorized Trading Remediation Program.
  • Royal Bank of Scotland. Managed the automation of P&L attribution, calculation and reporting based on risk sensitivities, using Lean and Agile methods.
  • ING Bank. Coordinated the testing cycles and the implementation of a business intelligence platform for value-at-risk (VaR) calculation and reporting.
  • Citibank. Received two Instant Recognition Awards for migrating mutual funds reconciliation, valuation and transactions control processes.
  • Methods and background. Works in agile delivery (Scrum, Kanban), structured project methods (PRINCE2) and Lean, and served as a Senior AI ambassador for HSBC Group. Master's degree in Law, University of Warsaw. English, Polish, Spanish.
